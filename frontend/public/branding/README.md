# ShelfPick branding assets

The approved source is the supplied `Cozy Board Game Shelf Icon.png`, retained
in this repository as `shelfpick-icon-source.png`. It contains three illustrated
board-game boxes, a gold meeple, a complete wooden shelf and a dark-green square
background. Do not regenerate, crop or substitute this source.

The simplified **Shelf + Meeple** mark echoes the same box colours, gold meeple
and wooden shelf. Its solid geometry is designed to survive favicon and in-app
wordmark reduction without imitating the source illustration.

## Exports

- `shelfpick-logo-light.svg`: simplified mark and Forest/Gold wordmark for light surfaces.
- `shelfpick-logo-dark.svg`: simplified mark and Cream/Gold wordmark for dark surfaces.
- `shelfpick-mark.svg`: transparent simplified mark; follows the operating-system colour scheme.
- `favicon.svg`: dark-green small-size mark used by browser tabs.
- `apple-touch-icon.png`: 180px uncropped export of the approved illustration.
- `pwa-192.png`, `pwa-512.png`: uncropped install-icon exports.
- `pwa-maskable-192.png`, `pwa-maskable-512.png`: padded exports keeping the
  boxes, meeple and shelf inside the central 80% safe circle.
- `social-profile-1024.png`: 1024px square social-profile export.

Use the full logo for authentication, onboarding and other deliberate brand
moments. Use the mark for icons, avatars and compact brand contexts. Do not put
the full wordmark into task navigation or repeat it beside visible `ShelfPick`
text. Keep the mark's clear space and do not display it below 16px.

Do not recolour, stretch, rotate, outline or add effects to the approved raster
artwork. The simplified vector assets use the reference's teal, orange, navy,
gold and wood cues; their wordmarks retain ShelfPick's established Forest,
Cream and Gold theme colours.
